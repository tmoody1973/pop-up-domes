## Drawings and documents

- `03-floor-plans/01`–`06` are figures from the National Register of Historic Places registration form for the Mitchell Park Horticultural Conservatory, prepared for the City of Milwaukee, 2026. Figure 4 (existing first-floor plan), Figure 5 (lower-level plan), Figure 6 (basement plan), Figure 7 (aerial context), Figure 3 (boundary site plan). Public document.
- `03-floor-plans/04_dome-structural-section-patent.jpg` is sheet 1 of US Patent 3,192,668, "Dome Building Construction", Donald L. Grieb, filed 13 March 1961, granted 6 July 1965. Public domain.
- `04-structure-details/01` and `02` are historic construction photographs reproduced in the same National Register nomination, credited there to the Milwaukee County Parks Historic Photograph Collection.
- `03-floor-plans/00_nrhp-nomination-full.pdf` is the complete nomination as filed with the city (Legistar file 252211).
- `03-floor-plans/07_schematic-site-diagram.png` was drawn for this pack from the documented dimensions and the plan figure. It is schematic, not a survey.

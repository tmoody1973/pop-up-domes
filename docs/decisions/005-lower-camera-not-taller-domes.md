# 005: Keep the true dome shape; lower the camera so it reads as pointed

- **Decision:** The paper domes keep the real measured profile. Near the domes, the camera drops to a lower angle so they read as the tall, pointed "beehives" people know.
- **Why this came up:** The domes matched the real profile within 1.8%, yet from the toy's high, looking-down camera they looked round and squat. People know the Domes from the ground, looking up, where the pointed top dominates.
- **Options:**
  1. Lower the camera near the domes. Cost: a camera change, and less of the overview "map" feel while you are near a dome.
  2. Stretch the domes taller and pointier, like the AI concept picture. Cost: no longer accurate; the measured-fidelity check would fail on purpose.
  3. Leave it. Cost: the subject's most recognisable feature reads weakly.
- **What we chose and why:** Option 1. Tarik's call, Claude's recommendation. The shape is a fact about the building; the camera is a choice about how we look at it. Fix the choice, not the fact.
- **What we gave up:** The steady top-down view everywhere; the camera now changes mood near the domes.
- **How we'll know if this was right:** Shown a screenshot near a dome with no label, someone who has visited calls it "the Domes" without hesitating.

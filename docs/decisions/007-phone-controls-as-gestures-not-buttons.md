# 007: On phones, reach the keyboard-only features with multi-finger taps, not on-screen buttons

- **Decision:** Day/dusk/night, camera view and music are reached on touch screens by tapping with 2, 3 or 4 fingers. No buttons on screen.
- **Why this came up:** On a phone, three features were keyboard-only (N, V, M) and simply unreachable. Portrait also cropped the scene to one paved circle.
- **Options:**
  1. Multi-finger taps, explained in the opening hint. Cost: hard to discover once the hint fades.
  2. Small corner buttons. Cost: breaks the project's "no panels, no buttons" look rule; easy to find.
  3. Leave phones keyboard-less. Cost: half the toy is missing on the device most people will open a link on.
- **What we chose and why:** Option 1. Tarik's call, from Claude's recommendation. It keeps the page a clean paper scene; the hint names the gestures.
- **What we gave up:** Discoverability. Anyone who misses the first hint will not find dusk mode.
- **How we'll know if this was right:** Hand the phone to someone who has not seen it; within a minute they have found dusk without being told.

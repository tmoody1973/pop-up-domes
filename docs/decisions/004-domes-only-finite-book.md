# 004: The whole book is the Domes; no generated countryside

- **Decision:** The pop-up book holds only Mitchell Park and the Domes. The random world generator from Papertrails is removed. Replaces decision 002.
- **Why this came up:** Decision 002 kept Papertrails' endless countryside around the park. Tarik's goal is an experience of the Domes, not a fantasy world with the Domes dropped into it.
- **Options:**
  1. Domes only, on one finite page spread with real edges. Cost: loses the "walk forever" charm of the original; we have to build page edges and stop the traveller at them.
  2. Keep the countryside around the park (decision 002). Cost: the hard seam problem where generated roads meet the park, a 96-tile limit in the generator, and a world that dilutes the subject.
  3. One spread per dome, turning the page between them. Cost: page-turn transitions to build, and you lose seeing the three domes together, which is the building's signature view.
- **What we chose and why:** Option 1. Tarik's call. Bonus: it deletes the riskiest engineering in the plan (the seam and the generator's limits) instead of solving it.
- **What we gave up:** Endlessness, and Papertrails' game layer (collectibles, atlas map), which assumed endless exploration and is switched off for now.
- **How we'll know if this was right:** Someone who has visited the Domes plays it for two minutes and wants to walk the whole site rather than leave the page.
